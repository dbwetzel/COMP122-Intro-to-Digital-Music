One-Shot Sampler

Sample meta-data editor by D. B. Wetzel (Sept. 2025) for COMP/MUSC 122 Intro to Digital Music (LUC). Version 2.0

Upload sound files to play them as "one-shot" or looping samples in an interactive system. Tag your samples with meta-data that defines how they play back. Generate a JSON data file that you can upload to this web app with your samples.
