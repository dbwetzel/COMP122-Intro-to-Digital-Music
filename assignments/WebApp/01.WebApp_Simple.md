# Web App #1 - Simple Player
*Create a free account at editor.pjs.org and make a copy of the "Simple Player" app*

## Instructions

1. Go to https://editor.p5js.org/ and **create an account** (free, no ads! they won't even email you!)
2. **Log in** to your new P5 editor account 
3. **Click on this link** to open my demo app: 
 - <a href="https://editor.p5js.org/dbwetzel/full/3R_7_GCXQ" target="_blank">https://editor.p5js.org/dbwetzel/full/3R_7_GCXQ </a>
 - It's a simple web app with a metronome and a palette of note and sample triggers.
 - We will play with it class and I'll show you how we can use it to play together!
4. **Make a copy.** Assuming you are logged in, go to the "File" menu and choose **"Duplicate"** <image src="images/P5_File_Duplicate.png" align="right" width=150 alt="File -> Duplicate">  
 - You should now have your own copy of the Simple Player app in your account
 - You could potentially add/delete sounds from your own version. More about that later (it's tricky!)
5. **Get a link** to your copy of the sketch: go to File->Share and click on the "Share Sketch As View-Only" link to copy it to the clipboard
 - Make sure that the title of the sketch says "Simple Player by \[YourName]" (not "by dbwetzel")
 - Make sure your username is in the link!
6. **Submit** the link to your copy of the Simple Player as your completed assignment!

It's very important that you complete this task as soon as possible so that I know you have a functional P5 editor account and I know your username. You do NOT need to modify the template/demo in any way, but feel free to play with it or even hack the code if you are so inclined (if you break it, you can always start over).
